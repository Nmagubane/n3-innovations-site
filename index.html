The first problem we solve

Mines already collect the warnings. Too often, they do not reach the right person in time.

Condition-monitoring reports, alarm logs, maintenance records and shift reports can point to a failure before it happens. Each sits in a different system, inbox or spreadsheet, so by the time someone connects them, the pump has failed or the shift has ended.

Each large vendor owns one source of data: the machines, the sensors, the historian or the work-order system. Nobody neutral owns the step between "a warning exists" and "the right job is done on time". That step is where we work.

How we work

We do not sell a platform and hope it fits. We measure the problem at your operation, prove the fix against a baseline, and then run it as a supported service.

What the research taught us

Technology wins in mining when it starts with a measured problem on site.

We studied 14 companies and projects, from Komatsu's autonomous haulage to platforms that spent billions before proving a single use case. These rules come from that work.

  • Problem first, product laterWe find and measure problems before we build anything.Lesson from GE Predix and Argo AI
  • Mining people in the roomWe work alongside each mine's own staff and experienced mining practitioners.Lesson from Plotlogic and KoBold Metals
  • One use case, proven, before a platformWe recommend one fix at a time, proven against a measured baseline.Lesson from GE Predix and Komatsu FrontRunner
  • No AI claims we cannot showEvery figure we report traces back to a data source or an interview at the mine.Lesson from Builder.ai and IBM Watson Health
  • Plan for integration and peopleLevel 9 shows that technology alone does not change outcomes.Lesson from Rio Tinto AutoHaul
  • Work with what the mine already hasAny fix must connect to the radios, networks and systems in use.Lesson from Newtrax
  • Earn trust on the floorWe brief organised labour and health and safety representatives before we start.Lesson from Nautilus Minerals

What our technology will never do

  • Control equipment, or write to PLCs and SCADA
  • Take the place of certified safety systems such as collision avoidance
  • Replace a mine's CMMS, historian or fleet system
  • Grow into a large platform before one module has proven its value
  • Use AI where a spreadsheet would do just as well
  • Make a claim we cannot show with the mine's own data
