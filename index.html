The problem we solve

Mines already have the data. It sits in separate systems, on paper and in spreadsheets, and it reaches the right person too late.

Each large vendor owns one source of data: the machines, the sensors or the work-order system. Nobody owns the step between "the data exists" and "the right person has acted, with the evidence to prove it". That step is what our platform does.
