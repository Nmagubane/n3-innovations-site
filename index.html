MHSA Chapter 8 · Level 9

Regulation already expects machines and people to communicate. Since December 2022, diesel trackless mobile machines must slow down and stop on their own when the operator does not act. A system like that is only as good as the information it acts on.

The problem we solve

Mines already have the data. It sits in separate systems, on paper and in spreadsheets, and it reaches the right person too late.

Machine data sits in one portal, diesel in a logbook, breakdowns on job cards and tonnes in a spreadsheet. By the time someone pieces it together, a machine has stood for days, money has leaked or a cost has crept up without anyone noticing.

Each large vendor owns one source of data: the machines, the sensors, the historian or the work-order system. Nobody owns the step between "the data exists" and "the right person has acted, with the evidence to prove it". That step is what our platform does.

Across the mine life cycle

From operating mines to closure and new projects.

The same data discipline that makes an operating mine safer and more productive also guides how a site is closed, rehabilitated and brought back into production.

    Operating mines and plants

    Our platform helps contract miners, operating mines and processing plants keep machines working, diesel accounted for and costs under control.

  2. Next · Close and rehabilitate

    Closure and rehabilitation

    Rehabilitating closed and derelict sites with specialist partners, planned on evidence, with progress that can be shown.

    The Department of Mineral and Petroleum Resources puts the closure cost of derelict and ownerless mine sites at about R51.5 billion, R8.6 billion of it for high-priority sites (parliamentary reply, October 2026).

    Who we work withMine owners, the state, and environmental and rehabilitation specialists.

    In time, we will develop mining opportunities ourselves, with a focus on mines under care and maintenance, where existing infrastructure lowers the cost of returning to production.

    Who we work withRights holders, investors and experienced mining partners.
