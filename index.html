Regulation already expects machines and people to communicate. Since December 2022, diesel trackless mobile machines must slow down and stop on their own when the operator does not act. A system like that is only as good as the information it acts on.

The first problem we solve

Mines already collect the warnings. Too often, they do not reach the right person in time.

Condition-monitoring reports, alarm logs, maintenance records and shift reports can point to a failure before it happens. Each sits in a different system, inbox or spreadsheet, so by the time someone connects them, the pump has failed or the shift has ended.

Each large vendor owns one source of data: the machines, the sensors, the historian or the work-order system. Nobody neutral owns the step between "a warning exists" and "the right job is done on time". That step is where we work.

  1. Q1

    Where does information fail?

    A call that does not get through, a report that arrives late, a warning nobody sees, a handover that drops a detail.

  2. Q2

    What does each failure cost?

    Lost production time, delayed response, repeat work, exposure to a hazard and hours of manual reporting, counted in the mine's own figures.

    The radios, networks, sensors, software and people that any fix depends on.

How we work

Assess, prove, then run. Every engagement is measured.

We do not sell a platform and hope it fits. We measure the problem at your operation, prove the fix against a baseline, and then run it as a supported service.

  1. Stage 1

    Assess

    On siteWith your teams

    We work alongside your people to find where information breaks down, and rank each problem by safety risk, production impact and cost to fix.

    You receiveA ranked problem register and a recommended first fix, with its baseline.

  2. Stage 2

    Prove

    One use caseMeasured baseline

    We deploy the fix on read-only data, with your own staff approving every action it recommends.

    You receiveA measured improvement against the baseline, valued with your finance team.

  3. Stage 3

    Run

    Per-site subscription

    The proven service runs on your site, connected to your maintenance and reporting systems, and extends to the next problem on the register.

    You receiveMonthly reporting on closure rates, lead times, false alarms and the rand value agreed with you.

Mines can start at any stage. Where a problem is already clear, we go straight to proving the fix.

Across the mine life cycle

From operating mines to closure and new projects.

The same data discipline that makes an operating mine safer and more productive also guides how a site is closed, rehabilitated and brought back into production.

  1. Operate

    Operating mines and plants

    Our services help operating mines, processing plants and contractors act on the warnings in their own data: fewer unplanned stoppages, faster response and lower cost per tonne.

    Operational diagnostics · Maintenance intelligence · Environmental compliance and water monitoring · Data integration · Connectivity · Energy analytics

    Who we work withMine and plant managers, engineering and maintenance teams, contractors.

  2. Close and rehabilitate

    Closure and rehabilitation

    We bring the same monitoring to closed and derelict sites: water, decant, subsidence and illegal access tracked and reported, so rehabilitation is planned on evidence and its progress can be shown.

    The Department of Mineral and Petroleum Resources puts the closure cost of derelict and ownerless mine sites at about R51.5 billion, R8.6 billion of it for high-priority sites (parliamentary reply, October 2026).

    Who we work withMine owners, the state, and environmental and rehabilitation specialists.

  3. Develop

    Mine development

    We assess and develop mining opportunities, with a focus on mines under care and maintenance, where existing infrastructure lowers the cost of returning to production.

    The Minerals Council has recommended opening derelict mines and the more than 30 mines under care and maintenance to junior miners (Daily Maverick, April 2026).

    Who we work withRights holders, investors and experienced mining partners.
