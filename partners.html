For partners and funders

A disciplined route from a measured mine problem to recurring revenue.

Our model is a software licence per operation. Each operation adds data, references and recurring revenue, and that revenue funds the move into rehabilitation and mine development.

  • NeedSafety and output are both slipping53 deaths between January and mid-October 2026, against 41 in all of 2025, and production down 12.7% year on year in August 2026.
  • MarketAI in mining is growing fastMarketsandMarkets forecasts growth from USD 2.60 billion in 2025 to USD 9.93 billion by 2032 (source).
  • CapitalSoftware, not hardwareWe work with data mines already collect, so each new operation is configuration, not capital equipment.
  • AccessDesigned to pass security reviewRead-only, outbound-only software gives a mine's IT and OT teams far fewer reasons to say no.
  • ChannelPartners, not rivalsEnvironmental consultancies and condition-monitoring providers already work on site. They keep their audit and monitoring work, and our platform gives them clean data, which makes them natural resellers.
  • RecurringLicences that renewThe platform becomes the operation's daily record of machines, costs, findings and actions, so licences renew and grow with each new site and module.
