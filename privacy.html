Privacy notice

How we handle your personal information.

This notice explains what personal information N³ Innovations collects through this website and when you contact us, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

This website has no contact form, no cookies and no advertising tools. We collect personal information only when you choose to contact us by email or phone: your name, contact details, organisation and what you tell us.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits. It sets no cookies and does not track individual visitors.

Our website host (GitHub Pages), our domain name provider (Cloudflare) and Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces, process technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site.

Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and discuss our services
  • To prepare proposals and agreements you ask for
  • To keep the business records the law requires

We do not sell personal information or use it for marketing without your consent.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run our email and website, under their data protection terms. Some of these providers store information outside South Africa. Where they do, we rely on the protections POPIA requires for transfers across borders.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it for the purpose you gave it to us, or as long as the law requires us to keep business records.

Your rights

Our PAIA manual explains how to request records we hold. You may also ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email us at qhubela_nk@live.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Information Regulator.
