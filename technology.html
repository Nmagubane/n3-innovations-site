Our technology

How our technology works, and how it stays secure.

Our software reads the warnings a mine already collects, ranks them by what they put at risk, and tracks each one until the work is done. It advises people. It never controls equipment.

Where AI earns its place

We use AI where it does work a person cannot do at scale, and plain software everywhere else.

TaskWhat the AI doesWhat stays plain software
Reading condition-monitoring reportsLanguage models extract the asset, fault mode, severity and recommended action from PDFs and free-text work orders.Storing the finding, its due date and who owns it
Matching tags to assetsFuzzy matching links names such as "Pump 4 DE bearing" to the right entry in the asset register.The asset register itself, which stays in the mine's CMMS
Checking a warning against plant dataAnomaly detection on historian tags (current, temperature, vibration) shows whether a trend supports the finding.Thresholds, alarms and trend charts
Writing the weekly summaryA draft summary of open risks, which a planner edits and approves.Overdue lists, closure rates and the rand calculation
  • Decision support onlyNo model writes to a PLC, SCADA setpoint or permit system.
  • Every output shows its evidenceThe page, trend or work order it came from.
  • A person approvesNothing becomes a work order without a named approver.
  • We publish our missesFalse-alarm rates and lead times are reported, not hidden.
  • Data stays where the mine allowsOn-site or South African hosting. We send no plant data to a public AI service without written consent.

Security and OT

Built to pass a mine's IT and OT security review.

A mine is not an office network, and a cyberattack can stop a plant. Our design keeps us out of the control layer entirely.

  • Read-onlyWe read copies and exports. We never write to plant systems.
  • No inbound accessThe site agent connects out. No vendor remote sessions are needed.
  • Out of the control layerNothing we install touches PLCs, SCADA or field devices.
  • Data sovereigntyHosting in South Africa or on the mine's own server, with the same software.
  • POPIA by designNames in work orders are pseudonymised on arrival. Access is by role.
  • Works offlineThe agent buffers data and syncs when the link returns.
