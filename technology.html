Our technology

How our technology works, and how it stays secure.

N³ Mining Intelligence brings an operation's records together, finds what needs attention and tracks every finding to an owner. It advises people. It never controls equipment.

Where AI earns its place

We use AI where it does work a person cannot do at scale, and plain software everywhere else.

TaskWhat the AI doesWhat stays plain software
Reading recordsTurns documents, photos and reports into structured records, so nobody re-types them.The record itself, its time and its source
Explaining findingsExplains in plain language what changed, why it matters and what it costs.The figures behind every finding
Drafting briefs and reportsDrafts the morning brief and monthly reports for a person to check and approve.Every number, total and rand calculation
Answering questionsAnswers questions about the operation in plain language, with the evidence attached.Who can see what, by role
  • Decision support onlyNo model writes to a PLC, SCADA setpoint or permit system.
  • Every output shows its evidenceThe page, trend or work order it came from.
  • A person approvesNo condition, result or work order is final without a named approver.
  • We count our missesFindings that turn out to be wrong are counted and reported to the customer, not hidden.
  • Data stays where the mine allowsOn-site or South African hosting. We send no operational data to a public AI service without written consent.

Security and OT

Built to pass a mine's IT and OT security review.

A mine is not an office network, and a cyberattack can stop a plant. Our design keeps us out of the control layer entirely.

ControlPLCs, SCADA, field devices
  • Read-onlyWe read copies and exports. We never write to plant systems.
  • No inbound accessThe site agent connects out. No vendor remote sessions are needed.
  • Out of the control layerNothing we install touches PLCs, SCADA or field devices.
  • Data sovereigntyHosting in South Africa or on the mine's own server, with the same software.
  • POPIA by designPersonal information is pseudonymised on arrival. Access is by role.
  • Works offlineThe field app and the site agent store data and sync when the link returns.

Lessons we build by

Technology wins in mining when it proves its value on site.

Fourteen companies and projects, from Komatsu's autonomous haulage to platforms that spent billions before proving a single use case, shaped the rules we build by.

  • Value firstEvery module is tied to a cost or a risk the operation already measures.Lesson from GE Predix and Argo AI
  • Mining people in the roomWe work alongside each mine's own staff and experienced mining practitioners.Lesson from Plotlogic and KoBold Metals
  • One module, proven, before the nextEach module proves its value at one operation before it extends to the next.Lesson from GE Predix and Komatsu FrontRunner
  • No AI claims we cannot showEvery figure we report traces back to the operation's own records.Lesson from Builder.ai and IBM Watson Health
  • Plan for integration and peopleLevel 9 shows that technology alone does not change outcomes.Lesson from Rio Tinto AutoHaul
  • Work with what the mine already hasAny fix must connect to the radios, networks and systems in use.Lesson from Newtrax
  • Earn trust on the floorWe brief organised labour and health and safety representatives before we go live.Lesson from Nautilus Minerals

What our technology will never do

  • Control equipment, or write to PLCs and SCADA
  • Take the place of certified safety systems such as collision avoidance
  • Replace a mine's CMMS, historian or fleet system
  • Roll out a module before it has proven its value at an operation
  • Use AI where a spreadsheet would do just as well
  • Make a claim we cannot show with the mine's own data
